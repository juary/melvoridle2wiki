A GOOD PLACE TO START

New to Melvor?

Melvor Idle 2 is a single-player idle RPG by Games by Malcs, published by Jagex. Train skills, gather resources and make progress at your own pace, including while offline.

Start with a skill that interests you, keep the resources you gather, and explore how they can help your next goal. For detailed game data and guides, visit the full wiki.
