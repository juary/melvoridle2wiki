# Sources and editorial scope

This project is an independent English-language guide to **Melvor Idle 2: A RuneScape Adventure**, developed by Games by Malcs and published by Jagex. The game entered Steam Early Access on **November 17, 2026**. It is not an official developer site.

Articles prioritize primary sources and link to the pages used for their claims. Review dates describe when this guide's material was checked; they are not game patch dates. Early Access balance, features, and content may change after a source was published.

## Editorial boundaries

- The guide does not invent recipes, unlock paths, item values, combat setups, quest routes, or exact progression times.
- Advice is labeled as a suggested approach, not a tested optimal build.
- Dated patch summaries describe their named release and do not guarantee that behavior remains unchanged.
- Early Access information is not automatically attributed to the separate demo.
- When developer announcements do not establish a detail, readers are directed to the current in-game interface.
- No prices, review totals, or player counts are treated as stable game facts.
